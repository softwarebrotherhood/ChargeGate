Lithium Battery Chemistry

Why does keeping your laptop at 100% kill it?

Modern laptops use high-density Lithium Polymer / Ion batteries. When kept at 100% on AC power, cell voltage is pushed to 4.35V+, accelerating cathode oxidation and permanent capacity degradation.

Standard Windows Behavior
Locked at 100% on AC
  • 4.35V – 4.40V continuous high-voltage strain
  • Electrolyte decomposition & cathode cracking
  • Degrades to ~70% health in just 12 to 18 months
  • Significant risk of battery swelling / pillowing
With ChargeGate
Gated at 80% with AC Passthrough
  • ~4.05V optimal resting cell voltage
  • Motherboard switches power entirely to wall adapter
  • Retains 90%+ health after 3–4 years of daily use
  • Near-zero heat generation and zero micro-cycling wear

Native BIOS / ACPI Control

ChargeGate communicates directly with your laptop's Embedded Controller via WMI, setting hardware thresholds in BIOS NVRAM.

⚡

Zero-Latency Optimistic UI

Instantaneous visual feedback with 0 ms lag. All hardware queries run asynchronously in the background so your app never freezes.

✈️

Smart Travel Mode

Need 100% for a flight? Click Travel Mode. ChargeGate will top you up to 100% and automatically revert back to 80% as soon as you unplug.

📊

Hardware Telemetry

Inspect real-time wattage draw ($W$), cell pack voltage ($V$), and true hardware cycle count reported by smart battery registers.

🎯

Dynamic Taskbar Tray Icon

See your live battery percentage badge right next to the Windows clock with color-coded status rings for active gating, charging, or discharging.

Lightweight & Ad-Free
